# Data Collection - How Is The Data Collected

## Daily Log

These data are recorded on a daily basis exactly from October 1, 2026.

### Sleep
Sleep duration is recorded in minutes.

### Exercise
Exercise refers to intentional physical activity. Activities like going for a walk are also included

### Screen Time
Screen time refers to non-study screen use.
Online courses, coding, research, and schoolwork are excluded.

### Study Time
Study time refers to active extracurricular study time.
Regular classroom attendance is excluded.

## Exam Results

Only formal school examinations are recorded.

Exam scores and grades are taken from official school records whenever available.

### Rankings and Class Sizes

Class and grade rankings, along with their corresponding cohort sizes, are recorded only when officially available.

Unavailable values are left blank. Missing rankings do not prevent other available exam results from being recorded.

## Data Quality

Data are recorded prospectively rather than reconstructed from memory.

Missing values are not fabricated. If a value cannot be reliably determined,
it is left missing or explicitly marked as estimated.
